## Collection process

1. Confirm the team's required participants, evaluation criteria, and submission method before requesting responses. The [team working agreement and role guide](TEAM_WORKING_AGREEMENT_DEFINITION_OF_DONE_ROLE_GUIDE.md) lists the project roles; it does not define a peer-rating scale.
2. Ask each participant to provide feedback for the agreed peers using a separate copy of the form below. Keep feedback behavior-based, specific, respectful, and focused on Sprint 4 collaboration and work.
3. Collect responses privately. Do not put named or raw peer feedback in this repository, a pull request, or a public demo.
4. Track receipt separately from evaluation content. Share only agreed, privacy-safe themes with the intended recipients; do not attribute comments without the author's permission.
5. If the team requires scores or a formal rubric, use the team's approved rubric rather than introducing a new scale here.
