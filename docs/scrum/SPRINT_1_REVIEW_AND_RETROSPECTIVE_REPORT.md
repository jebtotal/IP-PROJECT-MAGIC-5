## Retrospective

These are observations from the board and mid-sprint notes, not a substitute for each team member's feedback.

### What went well

- Development, database, and UI/UX tasks shown on the board are merged.
- The test plan and acceptance criteria were completed.
- Work was divided across clear team roles and tracked by task, owner, due sprint day, and status.

### What could be improved

- Many tasks did not meet their planned sprint dates.
- Several test tasks remain in progress or not started while many implementation PRs are already merged.
