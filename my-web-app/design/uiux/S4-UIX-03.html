Design rationale and handoff

  • The seven reports are sales summary/by customer/by product, customer directory, product catalog, employee roster, and job history.
  • Show selected filter scope and a no-results response; print output omits app navigation.
  • The audit viewer is a separate access-controlled design, not an eighth report.
